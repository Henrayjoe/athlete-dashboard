The read (Bucket 3)

One card per player, sorted most-concerning first. Flag is a z-score — how many standard deviations this match sits from the player's own norm. Green within ±1 SD, amber ±1–2, red beyond ±2. The shaded band on the trend is that ±1 SD normal zone. Compared to themselves, never the squad. Click a card to open the full GPS profile.

Match-only data: a red flag means "this is unusual for him — manage the following training week," not an injury verdict. Grey = fewer than 5 matches, the range isn't reliable yet.

Load engine (Bucket 2)

Match-to-match, per player. Int = HSR per minute (intensity, minutes-adjusted). Vol = total HSR (volume). Norm = rolling average of the player's last 5 matches. z = standard deviations from that norm (the flag basis). Ratio = latest ÷ norm, shown for reference.

A player needs 5+ matches before the z-score is reliable (flagged "building baseline"). This is the raw maths behind the colour flags in the read above.
